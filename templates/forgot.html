Reset your password

This app runs on your own laptop and has no email service, so resets are done on the laptop.

On the laptop that runs ErgoVest, open a terminal in the project folder and run:

python reset_password.py YOUR_USERNAME

It asks for a new password and signs that account out everywhere. Signed in already? Use Settings, then Change password.
